        "Scaling ensures that all numerical features contribute equally to a machine learning model's calculations. Many algorithms (like KNN, SVM, or Gradient Descent-based algorithms) compute the distance between data points. If one feature has a range of 1 to 10 and another ranges from 1,000 to 100,000, the algorithm will be disproportionately dominated by the feature with the larger scale, leading to poor performance and slower convergence. Scaling normalizes or standardizes these ranges so the model learns the true underlying patterns rather than just reacting to the magnitude of the numbers."
